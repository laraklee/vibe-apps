# Centrifuge Balance

## What it does

Students load a 6-slot centrifuge rotor and spin it. Tapping a slot cycles it
through empty → DNA sample → H₂O blank → empty. Each round's goal says how many
DNA samples to load (1, 2, 3 and 4, in random order).

- "CLOSE LID & SPIN" checks that the rotor is balanced (tubes spaced evenly
  around the center) and that the DNA count matches the goal.
- An unbalanced rotor shakes and shows "⚠️ UNBALANCED! CHECK POSITIONING".
- The last round requires at least one water blank if the student hasn't used
  one yet.
- Four successful spins earn "CERTIFIED LAB TECHNICIAN!". Nothing is saved, and
  refreshing the page starts over. No student data leaves the device.

Built to be embedded in Google Sites (transparent background).

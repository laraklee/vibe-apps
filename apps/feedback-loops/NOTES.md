# Feedback Loops: Body Loops, What Happens Next?

## What it does

A homeostasis game built around stimulus → sensor → effector. Each "mission"
starts the body away from its target. Students tap effector buttons to steer a
meter back to the normal checkpoint (or on to a goal). Then they classify the
loop as Negative or Positive feedback and see a short explanation.

Missions: hot P.E. class, freezing rain, high blood sugar, low blood sugar,
childbirth (positive), and severe shock in two parts (negative feedback, then a
positive-feedback downward spiral). Ends with a 7-question mastery quiz with
hints, then a score and streak screen. A "Jump" menu skips to any mission.

Score and streak live only on the page. Nothing is saved, and no student data
leaves the device.

## Class and unit

9th grade Biology, homeostasis and feedback loops (Lara to confirm the unit name).

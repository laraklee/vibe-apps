# Micropipet: Pipette Mastery Trainer

## What it does

Students read the volume window of a P20 or P200 micropipette and type in the
volume in μL. On the P20, the last digit (shown in red) is the tenths place, so
"053" means 5.3 μL. On the P200, all three digits are whole μL, so "053" means 53 μL.

- Two correct answers in a row on each pipette (four dots) earns "CERTIFIED!".
- A wrong answer shows the right volume and resets that pipette's dots to zero.
- Nothing is saved. Refreshing the page starts over. No student answers or scores
  leave the device.
